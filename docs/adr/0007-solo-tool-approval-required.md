# 0007 - Solo operator tool; no unapproved product changes

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Imperium is installed into systems the founder works on. It is not a
growth/SaaS push for strangers first.

## Decision

Imperium is the founder’s tool for systems he operates. Do not change
product direction, public positioning, or core contracts without his
explicit approval.

## Consequences

- Agents and contributors propose; the founder decides.
- “Ship for anonymous mass install” is not a default goal beyond stated
  beta gates in `GOALS.md`.
- brain-to-docs / ADR updates require his answers; do not invent taste.
